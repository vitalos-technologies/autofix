    Who can do what

    SuperAdmin
    Everything. Plan and status changes, job retries and domain re-checks need a reason, your authenticator, and a link to your Autofix platform admin (Settings → Users).
    Support
    Reads every page, sees customer names and masked contact details on a garage's bookings (each view is audited), and can hand off to View as support.
    Finance
    Plans, statuses, counts and the sign-up funnel. No personal data and no changes.

    How changes reach Autofix

    • This system reads Autofix through a read-only database role. It can call exactly three Autofix functions: change a garage's plan or status, retry a failed job, and queue a domain re-check. Each one checks you are a platform admin and writes Autofix's own audit log, which the garage's owners can see.
    • View as support records why you need it, then opens the garage in Autofix. The read-only session starts there, under your own Autofix sign-in and authenticator. No password, token or session is passed in the link.
    • Registrations, phone numbers and email addresses arrive masked. Message bodies, job payloads and addresses never leave Autofix.
    • Times are shown in Europe/London.

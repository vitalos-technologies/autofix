# Privacy notice for customers of [Garage name] (v1.0)

**Who we are.** [Garage legal name], [address], is responsible for your personal data ("we"). Contact us at [email] or [phone]. Our booking system, Autofix, is provided by Vitalos Technologies Ltd, which handles your data for us under a contract.

**What we collect**

- Your name, mobile number, email address and, for mobile visits, your address and postcode.
- Your vehicle registration and details about the vehicle, including MOT and mileage history from DVLA and DVSA.
- Booking details, notes you give us about the vehicle, and messages and calls between us. If we miss your call, our system records the number, the time and any voicemail you leave.
- Your reminder preferences.

**Why we use it, and our lawful basis**

| Purpose | Lawful basis |
| --- | --- |
| Taking and managing your booking, contacting you about it, invoicing | Contract |
| Looking up your vehicle so we quote and book correctly | Contract |
| Texting you back after a missed call so you can book | Legitimate interests (responding to your enquiry) |
| MOT and service reminders | Your consent, or, if you are an existing customer, our legitimate interests under the soft opt-in rules. You can opt out at any time |
| Keeping records for tax and accounting | Legal obligation |
| Preventing fraud and misuse of our booking page | Legitimate interests |

**Who we share it with.** Vitalos Technologies (our booking system provider) and the services it uses to run it: hosting, database, email, SMS and WhatsApp delivery, and error monitoring. If we offer an automated assistant on our website or phone line, your messages are processed by AI services to answer you. Vehicle registrations are checked with DVLA, DVSA and a vehicle data provider. Some of these providers process data outside the UK under approved safeguards.

**How long we keep it.** Booking and customer records while you are a customer and for up to 24 months after your last booking. Invoices for 6 years for tax purposes. Message content for up to 12 months.

**Your rights.** You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, object to how we use it, or ask us to restrict it. Reply STOP to any reminder text, or use the link in our messages, to stop reminders. Contact us using the details above.

**Complaints.** You can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

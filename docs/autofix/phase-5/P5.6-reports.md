# P5.6 Advanced reports and exports

Pro feature `advanced_reports` (D17: "advanced reports and exports: revenue by service and technician, bay utilisation, hours sold vs worked, reminder ROI"). Admin route `/app/reports`, CSV exports under `/api/admin/reports/<kind>`, and "Who did the job" on the booking page. Branch `phase-5/p5.6-reports`. Today's basic figures (Today, Reminders' "won back", `reminder_won_back()`) are untouched and stay on Essentials.

## Architecture

### Schema (`supabase/migrations/20261029000600_reports.sql`; pgTAP `032_reports`)

| Object | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| `booking_assignments` (tenant) | `garage_id` (default `current_garage_id()`), `booking_id` (composite FK → `bookings (garage_id, id)` on delete cascade, unique per garage), `technician_id → auth.users` (on delete set null), `technician_name` (snapshot, 1–120 chars, kept when the member leaves), `assigned_by`, `assigned_at`. RLS: members of the selected garage read (not plan checked, so readable after a downgrade); no staff write grants: written only by `assign_booking_technician()`. In `FIXTURES` of `tests/integration/tenancy.test.ts` |

No existing table, function or migration is changed. `create_booking*`, `available_slots*` and `staff_available_slots*` are not touched.

### SQL

All `security definer`, `search_path = ''`. Report dates are garage-local and inclusive; the window is `[from 00:00, to + 1 00:00)` in `garages.timezone`, so a clock-change day is 23 or 25 hours long. Errors: `42501 NOT_ALLOWED`, `P0001 PLAN_REQUIRED: advanced_reports`, `22023 INVALID_RANGE` (from > to, or more than 366 days).

| Function | Who | What |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `assign_booking_technician(booking uuid, technician uuid) → jsonb` | owners/managers of the selected garage (`garage_actor_allowed()`: AAL2 when enrolled), Pro | Sets (null: clears) who did the job. Technician must be a member of the garage (`22023 NOT_A_MEMBER`); booking must be the garage's and not trashed (`P0001 BOOKING_NOT_FOUND`). ## Assumptions

| # | Assumption | Risk |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A334 | **Revenue** counts bookings whose appointment (`starts_at`) falls in the garage-local dates, excluding `requested` (not yet agreed), `cancelled`, `no_show` and trashed bookings. A booking counts at the sum of its `issued`/`paid`, untrashed invoices (`total_pence`, VAT included as issued); with none, at `bookings.price_pence` (the snapshot), shown as "not invoiced yet" (`estimated_pence`); quote-only bookings with no invoice count £0. Invoices not linked to a booking (walk-in invoices) are not in these reports (Invoices has its own export). Future confirmed bookings in the range count at their price | Owners may expect revenue by invoice date instead of appointment date; switching is a change to `report_jobs()` only |
| A335 | **Technician**: a new `booking_assignments` table (one row per booking) rather than a `bookings` column, so the shared `bookings` table and its column grants stay as they are. Any member may be assigned (owners often do the work). Setting it is part of Pro (`advanced_reports`) because it exists only to feed the reports; on Essentials existing assignments stay readable on the booking page but can't be changed. Revenue is attributed wholly to the one assigned person; unassigned jobs are their own row | Garages wanting several technicians per job or split revenue need a different model (assignments per person with shares) |
| A336 | **Bay utilisation**: per resource (active ones, plus inactive ones with bookings in the range), per local day: open minutes = that weekday's `opening_hours` windows built in `garages.timezone` (so a window across 01:00–02:00 on a clock-change Sunday is an hour shorter/longer), 0 on a `closures` day; booked minutes = live bookings' `during` (requested included, as it holds the resource; buffers and van travel included) inside those windows only. Staff bookings outside opening hours are not counted, so use never exceeds 100%. Mobile vans and technician resources are listed too | Owners who book a lot outside hours will see lower use than the diary suggests |
| A337 | **Hours sold vs worked**: jobs = A334's bookings that reached `arrived` or later. Sold = `ends_at − starts_at` (the service duration snapshot; no buffer/travel). Worked from `booking_events` status changes, each status lasting until the next change: total time `in_progress` (time `waiting_parts` excluded); a job never marked In the bay counts its time `arrived` → next step. Only jobs that reached `ready`/`collected` with a start are measured; sold and worked are both over measured jobs. Invoices have no labour hours today, so they aren't used. "Sold vs worked" = sold ÷ worked (over 100% = quicker than sold) | Garages that don't tap through statuses get "Not measured"; clocking in/out per technician would be more accurate (future) |
| A338 | **Reminder return** follows `reminder_won_back()`: sent = sends with `sent_at` in range, per kind; bookings/pence = live, untrashed bookings credited to a send of that kind and *created* in range (invoice total, else price, else 0). Missed-call text-backs are not reminder sends and aren't listed | Same as the Essentials figure, so they agree |
| A339 | **SMS cost**: segments are the P5.2 `usage_events` rows with source `sms:reminder_send:<id>` for the sends counted. The cost per segment is a new placeholder env `SMS_SEGMENT_COST_PENCE` (decimals allowed); until set, the report shows text counts only. The plan allowance itself is not subtracted (texts within the allowance are shown at the same per-text cost, as a guide) | Cost model may differ once prices are set (Waiting on Temitope) |
| A340 | **Campaign ROI** is shown only when `public.campaign_sends` exists with `garage_id`, `sent_at` and `booking_id` (P5.5, built in parallel): same rules as A338 by dynamic SQL; otherwise `available = false` and the row is hidden. No dependency on the campaigns schema beyond those three columns | If P5.5 renames those columns, the row silently stays hidden; pgTAP 032 asserts `available` follows the table |
| A341 | Ranges: presets are this calendar month (whole month, including booked days ahead), last calendar month, last 90 days ending today; custom up to 366 days. Bad custom dates fall back to this month with a message. Exports use the same query (`range`, `from`, `to`) and are owners/managers only, like the page. Reports read with a remembered device (`allowTrustedDevice`), like Reminders; assigning needs the full two-step sign-in (`requireStaffWriter` + `garage_actor_allowed()`) | Low |

## Waiting on Temitope

- `SMS_SEGMENT_COST_PENCE`: the price per SMS segment to show in Reminder return (with the plan prices and allowances, D17). Until then only counts are shown.
- Nothing else: no keys, accounts or money involved.
